# 6. Government / Government Institution — Complete Logged-in Structure

For Government, the workspace should function as an **ecosystem intelligence + scheme/program management + funding + policy decision platform**.

Core flow:

**Register → Verify → Discover → Support → Allocate → Monitor → Measure Impact → Generate Policy Insights**

## Government's role inside StartupHub

The important distinction is that Government is **not just another startup-support user**.

The platform should give Government a higher-level view of the entire ecosystem:

**Startup Registry → Programs → Funding → Incubators → Resources → Startup Growth → Employment → IPR → Impact → Policy.**

This also creates the strongest connection between the other user roles: startups generate ecosystem data, incubators generate program data, investors generate investment data, mentors generate support data, and IPR experts generate IP data. The Government workspace can aggregate those signals into **ecosystem intelligence and evidence-based policy decisions**, subject to the appropriate permissions and data-sharing controls.
