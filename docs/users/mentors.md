### Core Mentor Flow

```text
Expert Profile
      ↓
Expertise + Availability
      ↓
Startup Discovery
      ↓
AI Startup Matching
      ↓
Mentorship Request
      ↓
Assigned Startup
      ↓
Sessions + Chat + Documents
      ↓
Assessment
      ↓
Recommendations + Tasks
      ↓
Milestone Review
      ↓
Progress Tracking
      ↓
Feedback
      ↓
Startup Outcome
      ↓
Mentorship Impact
```

This keeps the mentor experience substantially lighter than the Founder or Incubator workspace while still covering **all 20 requested features** and their necessary supporting pages.
