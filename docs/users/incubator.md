### Core incubator flow

```text
Applications
     ↓
Screening
     ↓
Evaluation
     ↓
Selection
     ↓
Cohort
     ↓
Startup Portfolio
     ↓
Assessment
     ↓
Roadmap + Milestones
     ↓
Mentors + Resources
     ↓
Funding + IPR + Market Support
     ↓
Performance Monitoring
     ↓
Risk Detection
     ↓
Intervention
     ↓
Outcome
     ↓
Program Analytics
```

This gives the incubator a clear **"manage → support → monitor → intervene → measure"** workflow while keeping the sidebar clean.
