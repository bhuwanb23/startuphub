# 9. University / Research Institution — Complete Logged-in Structure

For the University / Research Institution, StartupHub should function as a research discovery + technology transfer + IP commercialization + lab infrastructure + startup spin-off + industry collaboration platform.

Core flow:

Research → Discover → Protect IP → Validate Technology → Transfer / License → Create Spin-offs → Collaborate → Commercialize → Track Impact

```
   └──────┬─────────┘

          ↓
COLLABORATION & TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER
          ↓
COMMERCIALIZATION
          ↓
REVENUE + INNOVATION IMPACT
```

Role in StartupHub: Universities and research institutions become the ecosystem's research-to-market bridge, connecting faculty, students, research outputs, intellectual property, laboratories, startups, corporates, and funding sources. Their central outcome is turning research into commercially viable technologies, licensed IP, and research-driven startups.
