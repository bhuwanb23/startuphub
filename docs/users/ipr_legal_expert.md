## Core IPR Expert Flow

```text
Professional Profile
        ↓
Client / Startup Discovery
        ↓
IP Discovery
        ↓
IP Assessment
        ↓
Prior-Art Search
        ↓
Patentability Assessment
        ↓
IP Strategy
        ↓
Protection
   ┌────┼────┬────┐
Patent Trademark Copyright Design
   │
Trade Secrets
        ↓
Filing / Case Management
        ↓
Portfolio Management
        ↓
Deadline + Renewal Monitoring
        ↓
Risk Assessment
        ↓
IP Valuation
        ↓
Licensing / Transfer
        ↓
Commercialization
```

This makes the IPR workspace distinctly different from the founder's **IPR Management** module: the founder manages **their own IP**, while the IPR expert manages **multiple clients, cases, filings, portfolios, legal workflows, deadlines, and commercialization activities**.
