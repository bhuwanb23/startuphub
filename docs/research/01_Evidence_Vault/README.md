## The rule

> **Nothing here is ours. Retrieval only.**
> Interpretation and analysis belong in the topic folders (`03_Problem_Evidence/`, `04_Competitor_DeepDive/`, etc.) and must cite a `Source_Registry.csv` ID.

This separation is what makes the research auditable — and it is what lets us overturn an assumption later without rewriting the evidence.

## Highest-priority extraction

**The NIRF institution-wise DPIIT startup corpus.** Factly/Dataful extracted **6,817 rows across 204 institutions** from publicly-posted NIRF Data Capture System PDFs, and monetised it.

Three conclusions follow:
1. **The elite segment is real and identifiable today** — 204 institutions produced ~5,490 distinct recognised ventures over three cycles. Highest startup density, highest willingness to pay, most reachable. **The most reachable proof point in the entire census.**
2. **The data is machine-extractable** — institutions publish their own submitted NIRF forms on the open web (verified at NIT Rourkela, IIM Goa, JBIET, Rajah Serfoji Government College).
3. **Someone will build the data layer before a government body does.** Factly already has. That is both proof of concept for our own data asset and a warning that pure data play is partly occupied.
