## How to Choose Accelerator Software

![Article image](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcms-cdn.innoloft.com%2FScalerrs_36_How_to_choose_18cd2574f3.png&w=3840&q=75)

Pick tools with admin reality in mind. You need flexible workflows, clear visibility, and proof of results, not shiny dashboards or one-off forms that break mid cohort.

### 1) Application Management and Reviews

Start with strong Application Management. Your team needs pipelines that mirror stages, editable application workflows, and reliable Document Management. Reviews should support role-based access, structured scoring, and an audit trail.

-   Must have: kanban-style pipelines for application processes, changeable forms, bulk actions, and file handling

-   Nice to have: rubric libraries, reviewer assignments, and time-bound windows

-   Red flags: rigid flows, hidden exports, or “manual reviews” outside the system

### 2) Cohort Engagement, Events, and Networking

You will run sessions, ticketing, check in, and mentoring at once. Look for clean admin User Experience, schedules, attendance, and real matchmaking, not just chat.

-   Must have: event setup, ticketing, on-site check in, directories, and mentor matching

-   Nice to have: automated reminders, session feedback, and notes tied to profiles

-   Tie-in: plan purposeful sessions with proven community engagement activities and build a year-round hub using our how to build community guide

### 3) Proof of Impact, Security, and Support

Sponsors expect evidence. Track meetings, milestones, and outcomes with Impact Measurement and real time progress tracking you can export. Keep access secure and get help fast.

-   Must have: SSO, roles and permissions, exportable reports, and responsive Customer Service

-   Nice to have: API or CSV syncs, consent controls, and presentation-ready summaries

-   Red flags: vanity metrics without evidence, slow support, or data you cannot get out
