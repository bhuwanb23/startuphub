# P-A04 - One coordinator is the integration point for several unrelated national schemes

**Evidence grade:** `DOCUMENTED` - Multiple primary documents read and cited by corpus_id.

**Severity:** `HIGH` - Recurring, substantial time loss or error rate.

**Corpus documents cited:** `IIC-016`, `IIC-025`, `IIC-013`, `IIC-028`

> **Not an observed user problem.** Phase 3 user interviews have not been conducted. This is documentary analysis of what institutions publish about themselves. Do not present it to a third party as an observed user problem.

---

## Statement

Institutions are simultaneously running IIC, YUKTI-NIR, MSME Idea Hackathon, Smart India Hackathon, ATL and state innovation missions - each with its own portal, vocabulary and selection rules - through a single coordinator.

## Evidence

- CMRIT published a full YUKTI funnel for 2024-25: 50 PoCs + 70 innovations + 7 startups submitted, 162 ideas recommended after scouting, 10 forwarded to the national challenge. (`IIC-016`)
- Vijaya Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences ran the same year through three schemes with markedly different selectivity: Idea Hackathon 4.0 forwarded 12 of 17 ideas received (71%); Idea Hackathon 5.0 recommended 29 of 59 (49%); YUKTI recommended 5 of 15 submissions (33%). One coordinator, three screens, three different rates. (`IIC-025`)
- St Paul's College ran IIC, SIH, YUKTI and MSME Idea Hackathon 3.0 and 4.0 in one year, with 11 SIH teams and ~15 YUKTI products. (`IIC-013`)
- Rathinam College of Pharmacy's activity register shows MIC-driven, IIC-calendar, self-driven and celebration activities all tagged from one fixed five-value vocabulary regardless of which scheme they serve. (`IIC-028`)

### What is still missing

The corpus shows the multiplicity and the differing selectivity rates. It does not show the coordinator's time cost, nor how many schemes a typical institution runs. Both need Phase 3.

## Who experiences it

The IIC coordinator / Innovation Activity Coordinator. Evidence they are a single point: SSCET names a compiler, St Paul's names an Innovation Activity Coordinator as the report author, Scottish Church names the same role.

## Severity - HIGH

Multiple mandatory destinations, each with its own schema, funneled through one part-time role. Directly produces the P-A02 contradictions.

## Frequency

Continuous, and spikes at each scheme's submission deadline, which do not align.

## Current workaround

Tracking each scheme separately, then manually reconciling at the point where an idea or startup is submitted to more than one - and manually preventing double-counting across schemes.

## Quantified cost

Not measured. Observable proxy: CMRIT submitted 127 items across three YUKTI categories and forwarded 10 - an 92% rejection rate, meaning most coordinator effort in scouting and documentation produced no forwardable submission.

## Failure consequence

Double-submission of the same invention across schemes, or a missed deadline in one scheme because attention went to another. Both carry scheme-level consequences.

## Potential fixer

One innovation object with per-scheme submissions as derived views - so the same invention can legitimately appear in several schemes without being re-entered or double-counted.

## Competitive implication

The Phase 0 thesis already identified YUKTI-NIR / IIC / ARIIA export as a core differentiator. This is the documentary backing for it: the multiplicity is real and the selectivity rates differ per scheme.
