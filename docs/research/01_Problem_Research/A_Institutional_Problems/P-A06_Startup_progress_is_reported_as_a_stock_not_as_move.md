# P-A06 - Startup progress is reported as a stock, not as movement over time

**Evidence grade:** `SINGLE-SOURCE` - One primary document. Suggestive; must not carry a claim alone.

**Severity:** `HIGH` - Recurring, substantial time loss or error rate.

**Corpus documents cited:** `IIC-007`, `IIC-032`, `IIC-033`

> **Not an observed user problem.** Phase 3 user interviews have not been conducted. This is documentary analysis of what institutions publish about themselves. Do not present it to a third party as an observed user problem.

---

## Statement

No institution in the corpus reports how its startups are doing. Reporting is a count at a point in time, and several institutions cannot even produce the count.

## Evidence

- Of 36 documents, the startup field resolves to a number in only a minority; the rest use seven distinct expressions of absence or incompletion: Nil, NA, 'In process', 'In-progress', a dash, or an implied blank.
- ICFAI University Tripura is the only institution that separates startups established (0) from startups in pipeline (5) - i.e. the only one treating progress as two states rather than one number. (`IIC-007`)
- Brainware University: 91 patents published, 8 granted, 0 startups - a decade-scale research output with no reported venture formation at all. (`IIC-032`)
- Women's Christian College ran 84 activities and reports startups as 'In process' with no further detail. (`IIC-033`)

### What is still missing

The absence is well evidenced; the CAUSE is not. A null startup count could mean no startups, or no counting. Phase 3 must distinguish these, and this is the single most important question for the P-A01 thesis.

## Who experiences it

IIC coordinator; investors doing diligence on the institution's incubation claim; ARIIA assessors.

## Severity - HIGH

Rated above the mentorship problem because the evidence base is stronger and the consequence is more concrete: an institution actively running programmes reports nothing measurable about whether any of it worked.

## Frequency

Annual, at reporting time.

## Current workaround

UNKNOWN from documents. Cannot be stated without interview evidence.

## Quantified cost

Not available.

## Failure consequence

An institution cannot demonstrate outcome to an investor, a funding agency or an accreditation body, because it never captured the data.

## Potential fixer

Status and milestone history per venture, with an explicit 'unknown' state distinct from zero.

## Competitive implication

Every competitor surveyed in Phase 0 tracks intake and programme milestones, not venture survival or revenue. Outcome tracking over time is unclaimed ground - but it can only be sold if Phase 3 confirms institutions care about it.
