## Why the entry price matters so much

At **₹75,000** the sale splits across institutions: a solo officer can act at some, a committee is engaged at others. At **₹3,95,200** the picture inverts — the sale crosses into committee-and-escalation territory at most.

The same table also shows why a *uniform* national price cannot be optimised: the correct price for IIT Palakkad and for NIT Warangal are not the same number.

**Interaction with D-008.** The Decision Log holds the pricing floor above free-tier anchors (Trello ~₹54k/yr, ClickUp ~₹76k/yr, Notion ~₹86k/yr). Phase 2 says that floor sits *above* the solo-officer ceiling at most institutions measured (₹10k–₹25k), so StartupHub should **expect a committee, not a rubber stamp, even at the entry tier** — and should plan the sale accordingly rather than assuming a fast, cheap close.

## Six procedural facts that change the ladder

### GeM availability bypasses the purchase committee

*NIT Warangal's manual applies the Local Purchase Committee to purchases 'only on each occasion which are not available in GeM portal'. A GeM-routed purchase does not trigger the committee.* — `P2-S03` `SINGLE-SOURCE`

**Consequence.** If StartupHub lists on the Government e-Marketplace, a potentially committee-bound sale becomes a direct purchase at institutions that adopt this carve-out. Requires Phase 24 legal work to confirm the carve-out is general, not NITW-specific.

### Purchases above Rs 2 lakh must already be budgeted - private segment

*Sathyabama: 'All the Major Purchases (above 2 Lakhs) shall be included in the budgetary proposals of the department, duly approved by the Finance Committee, Board of Management before heading to the purchase procedure.'* — `P2-S06` `SINGLE-SOURCE`

**Consequence.** Above roughly Rs 2 lakh in this segment the purchase can only be funded from an existing, pre-approved budget line. It cannot be created mid-year. This is the strongest documentary support for D-007 - sell against an existing grant, do not create a new budget line.

### Committee membership can be blocked by one member

*ICAR-WII: SPC-I quorum requires 'Chairman, FO or IAO' plus HoD and Administrative Officer; SPC-II quorum requires Chairman, FO or IAO, one HoD and Administrative Officer. Any dissent by a finance member goes to the Director.* — `P2-S08` `SINGLE-SOURCE`

**Consequence.** A Finance Officer or Internal Audit Officer can stall or block a purchase alone. Role C-03 is a genuine veto player, not a reviewer.

### The indenter advises but does not vote

*ICAR-WII: the 'concerned indentor/project leader or officer nominated by the Project Leader shall be co-opted as a member of the committee... The co-opted member can only advise the committee.'* — `P2-S08` `SINGLE-SOURCE`

**Consequence.** The IIC Convener, as indenter, gets a voice but no vote at ICAR-WII. Contrast IIT Palakkad above Rs 25 lakh where the Convener sits on the EPC as a full member. Being the indenter does not confer decision rights.

### Splitting a demand to dodge a threshold is prohibited

*GFR 2017 Rule 157: demand for goods 'should not be divided into small quantities to make piecemeal purchases to avoid the necessity of obtaining the sanction of higher authority required with reference to the estimated value of the total demand.'* — `P2-S10` `DOCUMENTED`

**Consequence.** Closes the workaround of licensing per department to stay under a threshold. Any multi-year or multi-seat deal will be assessed on total annual value.

### A purchase order is not issued until audit approves

*IIT Palakkad: purchases above Rs 10 lakh are presented to Audit for perusal and approval; 'Upon receipt of the approval from the Audit for the purchase, the PO/WO is issued.'* — `P2-S02` `SINGLE-SOURCE`

**Consequence.** Audit is a hard gate, not advisory, at that value. Relevant only if StartupHub ever sells institution-wide rather than per-IIC.

## Honest limits

- **These eight are not a sample.** They were found on public institution websites, which likely favours well-governed, publication-friendly institutions. The true national spread is **at least** 20x, probably wider. Logged as OQ-25.
- **No national HEI procurement standard was found.** GFR 2017 binds central institutions, but private universities set their own policy under UGC norms, and these two private instruments differ sharply from the central ones.
- **Threshold is not behaviour.** A rule says what *may* happen. What happens is unobserved and is Phase 3's job.
- **Software is not always 'non-consumable'.** NIT Warangal separates the two, and the classification changes the ceiling. Untested elsewhere.
