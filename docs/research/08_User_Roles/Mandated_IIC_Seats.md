# The 12 Mandated IIC Seats

These are not personas. They are **offices the IIC portal refuses to let an institution operate without.**

## The gate, quoted

> *"If you delete the existing "Convener" / "YUKTI coordinator" / "Social Media coordinator" / "Innovation Activity coordinator" / "Internship activity coordinator" / "IPR activity coordinator" / "Startup activity coordinator" / "ARIIA coordinator" / "NIRF coordinator" / "Member" role details the sub-tab will become as "red cross". Because all the above mentioned roles are required to make this sub-tab to stay in "green tick". If it is in "red cross" then you cannot add any activity under "Manage Activity" section."*
>
> — MIC IIC portal, *Add/Update Faculty Member Details* `P2-S01`

Two consequences follow, and they matter commercially:

1. **Every IIC has someone in every one of these seats.** The population is not hypothetical — it exists, with named holders, right now.
2. **Seats are assigned by category, not by workload.** The Social Media Coordinator is a mandated office with no innovation-record workload. So the *number of IIC seats is not a proxy for seats that would use innovation software*.

The portal also constrains stacking: *"Try not to assign any extra roles for President and Convener alone, for other faculty you can assign two roles at max."* That concentrates the operational load on the Convener, who is explicitly told not to hold anything else.

## Who actually fills them

From the institutional constitutions read:

- **IIT Hyderabad** — the President is the **Director**. The Vice President is the **Dean (R&D)**. The Startup Activity Coordinator is the **Faculty-in-Charge of the Incubation Centre**. The IPR coordinator is the **Faculty-in-Charge of the IP Cell**. The Social Media Coordinator is the **Public Relations Officer**. `P2-S11`
- **Scottish Church College** — the 2024-25 report names President Dr. Samrat Bhattacharjee, Convener Mr. Saptarshi Chakraborty, and states it was *compiled by* **Dr. Tamanna Sultana, Innovation Activity Coordinator** — evidence that the compiling office is distinct from both President and Convener. `P2-S12` `P1-008`

**The IIC president is frequently the head of the institution.** That is why Class A is not the buying centre: the office owns the *data*, but the person holding it is often above the IIC entirely and delegating.

## The churn problem

Some coordinator seats are held by students. At IIT Hyderabad the Internship Coordinator is a **BTech 4th year** and the IPR Coordinator an **MTech 3rd year** student; the student-held Startup Activity Coordinator is Head of the E-Cell. `P2-S11`

So one human can simultaneously be a mandated officeholder and an end user — and will graduate. Phase 0 recorded the consequence: *"The person who would champion StartupHub graduates in 2–3 years; the person who signs is a faculty member who may transfer."*
**Licensing implication:** per-seat licensing is structurally fragile here. The institution is the durable unit; the seat is not.

## What this class is good for

It is a **complete, enumerable, already-populated seat list with published job descriptions** — a far better starting point than a persona exercise, because every duty below was written by the Ministry of Education, not invented here.
