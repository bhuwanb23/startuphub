## Segment 1 — Central / government institutions

Instruments read: Central Rajasthan State University, Delhi University, ICAR - Indian Institute of Rice Research, IIT Palakkad, NIT Karnataka, NIT Warangal.

| Question | Answer | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **Uses** | IIC Convener + the activity coordinators. Portal-enforced, with published duties. | `DOCUMENTED` |
| **Decides** | Convener inside the council; **Registrar / Dean** inside the institution; **Purchase Committee** on the merits | `SINGLE-SOURCE` |
| **Pays** | Budget authority sits with **Director** (all cases), **Deputy Director / Registrar / Dean (R&D)** (₹10 lakh non-consumable), and **HoD** (₹2.5 lakh) at NIT Warangal. **No IIC seat is documented to hold any of these.** | `DOCUMENTED` for the four offices; `HYPOTHESIS` for every Class A seat |
| **Approves** | Registrar/Dean (ICSR) for administrative approval; VC/Director above their ceiling; **Audit above ~₹10 lakh, where the PO is not issued until audit approves** | `DOCUMENTED` |

## Segment 2 — Private / deemed universities and colleges

Instruments read: Mangalore Institute of Technology & Engineering, Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology.

| Question | Answer | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **Uses** | Same offices — the IIC role set is imposed centrally regardless of sector | `DOCUMENTED` |
| **Decides** | **Principal** convenes the Purchase Committee at MITE; **Trust Chairman** chairs it; **Board of Management + Finance Committee** above ₹2 lakh at Sathyabama | `SINGLE-SOURCE` |

## Three asymmetries that should shape the GTM

### 1. The user cannot authorise the purchase

Phase 0 recorded this from competitor analysis: *"Their buyer is the Principal/Registrar. The IIC nodal officer — StartupHub's actual buyer — has no seat in that buying decision."* The procurement manuals now confirm it structurally: the IIC Convener's signature appears only as the **indenter** on a requisition, and at ICAR-WII the co-opted indentor *"can only advise the committee."* `P2-S08`

### 2. The person who signs has no reason to care about innovation records

The Registrar/Dean cares about procurement risk, precedent and budget period. They do not own innovation data. This means the champion and the approver are answering to different motivations, and the product must survive both.

### 3. The approver has one question StartupHub must pre-empt

"Is there an existing budget line for this?" Above roughly ₹2 lakh in the private segment the answer is structurally *no* — the line had to exist before the conversation started. `P2-S06` This is D-007 restated as a procurement fact rather than a sales opinion.

## What Phase 3 must settle

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Does the IIC Convener hold a budget line, or does the Dean/R&D office hold it and the Convener merely spends from it? | The single most important unknown in Phase 2. Determines who the economic buyer actually is. |
| Who actually initiates the requisition in practice — the Convener, the TBI manager, or the Dean? | Determines whether StartupHub has an internal champion or only a user. |
| Is the Convener and the Incubation Manager the same person? | MIC guidance says they should be. If true, the buying centre is one person, not two. |
| Do founders use the institution's systems at all? | `P-A01` found 38/55 startups unattributed. If founders are not users, the product is a single-sided compliance tool. |
