# A-01 — IIC President

**Class A** — Mandated IIC seats - portal-enforced. No activity can be entered without them.

| Question | Answer | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **Who uses it** | Yes, ultimately. Owns the portal login and is custodian of uploaded data, so is the account of record even where day-to-day entry is delegated to the Convener. | `DOCUMENTED` |
| **Who decides** | Likely the internal decision-maker for adopting a tool, because they can change council membership and monitor deliverables. But no source establishes that the President decides anything about procurement. | `SINGLE-SOURCE` |
| **Who pays** | UNKNOWN. No source states the President holds or controls a budget line. | `HYPOTHESIS` |
| **Who approves** | UNKNOWN for the purchase itself. At IIT Palakkad the administrative approval of a purchase is Registrar/Dean(ICSR), not the IIC President. | `HYPOTHESIS` |

## Mandated responsibility

> Holds the IIC portal Login ID and is "custodian of IIC portal login and data/ reports uploaded therein". Constitutes the council, chairs quarterly council meetings, is the main point of contact with the MHRD Innovation Cell, and is "responsible for submitting the monthly progress/activity reports on the IIC portal".

## Why this role matters

The strongest asset in Class A: a named officeholder who is formally the custodian of the data a replacement product would own. But custodian-of-data is not budget authority.
