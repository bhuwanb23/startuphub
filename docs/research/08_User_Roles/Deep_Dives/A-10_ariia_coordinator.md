# A-10 — ARIIA Coordinator

**Class A** — Mandated IIC seats - portal-enforced. No activity can be entered without them.

| Question | Answer | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **Who uses it** | Yes, and most acutely. ARIIA demands patent application numbers, year and commercialisation figures. Phase 0 established (D-004) that institutions therefore maintain a SEPARATE off-platform patent ledger purely to answer this screen. | `DOCUMENTED` |
| **Who decides** | Advises the council on rank improvement. | `SINGLE-SOURCE` |
| **Who pays** | UNKNOWN. | `HYPOTHESIS` |
| **Who approves** | No. | `HYPOTHESIS` |

## Mandated responsibility

> "Primary responsible for creating repository and advising on more innovation and startup activities in campus to increase the ranking in ARIIA." Marked optional in the institutional charters but present in the MIC portal role list.

## Why this role matters

Owns the repository. This is the role whose job description is literally "creating a repository" - i.e. the role that already does by hand what a system of record would do. Deep-dived in Decision_Map.
