# C-01 — Registrar / Dean (ICSR)

**Class C** — Off-portal gatekeepers - control the purchase; absent from the IIC portal and from todo.md's list. Added per D-019.

| Question | Answer | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **Who uses it** | No. Appears in this map only as an approver. Phase 0 already recorded the sharpest version of this: a competitor's analysis concludes "Their buyer is the Principal/Registrar. The IIC nodal officer - StartupHub's actual buyer - has no seat in that buying decision." | `SINGLE-SOURCE` |
| **Who decides** | Yes. CRSU vests administrative and financial approval above Rs 2,00,000 in the VC AFTER the Registrar's own ceiling of Rs 2,00,000 - making the Registrar the decision point for the middle band. | `DOCUMENTED` |
| **Who pays** | YES - DOCUMENTED. NIT Warangal: Registrar Rs 10,00,000 non-consumable, Rs 5,00,000 consumable. CRSU: Registrar up to Rs 2,00,000. UoH: Registrar Rs 1,50,000 without pre-check, Rs 5,00,000 with Purchase Committee recommendation. | `DOCUMENTED` |
| **Who approves** | Yes. This is the role that signs. In every institution measured, the IIC Convener recommends and the Registrar or Dean administratively approves. | `DOCUMENTED` |

## Mandated responsibility

> Grants administrative approval for purchases and holds delegated financial limits. At IIT Palakkad the 'Administrative Approval of the Registrar / Dean (ICSR) for the Purchase' is a required document in the audit pack above Rs 10 lakh.

## Why this role matters

Added per D-019. Deep-dived because it is the most probable approver of a StartupHub-scale purchase. The uncomfortable finding: the person who will sign has no reason to care about innovation records and every reason to care about procurement risk and precedent.
