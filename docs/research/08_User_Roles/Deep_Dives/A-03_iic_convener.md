# A-03 — IIC Convener

**Class A** — Mandated IIC seats - portal-enforced. No activity can be entered without them.

| Question | Answer | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **Who uses it** | Yes - the primary operator. Day-to-day activity, data collection from members, coordination across departments, and compilation of the annual report in practice. | `DOCUMENTED` |
| **Who decides** | Yes, within the council. Cannot change council membership (President can) but controls what is presented, and effectively sets the quarterly agenda. | `DOCUMENTED` |
| **Who pays** | UNKNOWN, and this is the single most important unknown in Phase 2. Every source read describes authority to SPEND money; none describes a budget line to spend FROM. The Convener is frequently a part-time academic - Phase 1 found SSCET fielding 40 IIC members, 30 innovation ambassadors and 2 mentors (P1 corpus IIC-024). | `HYPOTHESIS` |
| **Who approves** | Not the purchase approver. But at IIT Palakkad, purchases above Rs 25 lakh go to an Exclusive Purchase Committee whose composition explicitly includes the "Indenter (Convenor)" - so at high spend the Convener gets a formal vote. | `SINGLE-SOURCE` |

## Mandated responsibility

> "This is more responsible role, the person who is assigned to this role should carry out the day to day activity under IIC." Describes self as "Immediate point of contact and coordination" and "Preferably Incubation in charge of institute or Faculty with Entrepreneurship Experience or interest." Collects inputs from all council members at regular intervals. The portal instructs that no extra roles be assigned to Convener.

## Why this role matters

The most probable champion and the most probable first user. The user-decides-pays split for this role is the central open question of Phase 2. Portal guidance forbids stacking extra roles onto Convener, which concentrates the operational load.
