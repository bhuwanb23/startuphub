### H-01

**Claim.** Institutions have no venture register; StartupHub's portfolio is the first.

- **Graded:** HYPOTHESIS
- **Source:** P-A01 / P-A06 (documentary, 36-doc corpus)
- **Tested in:** Guide `G-B`, question 1 — Registrar / Dean (ICSR) / Controller of Examinations-adjacent office

**Refuting observation.** A Convener opens an existing system - institute ERP, a student information system, or a maintained shared sheet - that already lists their startups with names, stages and dates.

**If refuted.** The wedge is 'one place to see it', not 'first record'. Pricing and moat both weaken.

---

### H-02

**Claim.** The NIRF DPIIT join is broken because the officer lacks the number.

- **Graded:** SINGLE-SOURCE
- **Source:** F-DPIIT-JOIN (DOCUMENTED, 7 forms, 32/354 measured)
- **Tested in:** Guide `G-A`, question 2 — IIC Convener / Coordinator / ARIIA-NIRF nodal officer

**Refuting observation.** The Convener says they receive a ready list of DPIIT numbers with stages already filled, from the institute or a state portal.

**If refuted.** The join failure is a form-design artefact nobody actually feels, which removes the Phase 3A headline as a product driver.

### H-03

**Claim.** Excel / Sheets / WhatsApp are the free incumbent StartupHub displaces.

- **Graded:** HYPOTHESIS
- **Source:** Phase 0 assumption - NEVER EVIDENCED
- **Tested in:** Guide `G-A`, question 6 — IIC Convener / Coordinator / ARIIA-NIRF nodal officer

**Refuting observation.** The institute already pays for a system that does this work, or the work is done on the IIC portal itself and costs nothing either way.

**If refuted.** There is no free incumbent; the comparison set is wrong and the whole positioning is mis-aimed.

### H-04

**Claim.** Coordinated filing deadlines are the forcing event that creates urgency.

- **Graded:** HYPOTHESIS
- **Source:** D-007 (Phase 0 inference) / P-A02
- **Tested in:** Guide `G-A`, question 4 — IIC Convener / Coordinator / ARIIA-NIRF nodal officer

**Refuting observation.** The work is done continuously and the deadline simply marks when it is submitted - no deadline pressure, no rework, no overtime.

**If refuted.** There is no forcing event, so there is no trigger to sell against. Timing strategy must change entirely.

### H-05

**Claim.** The Convener is the buyer, or can reach the buyer directly.

- **Graded:** HYPOTHESIS
- **Source:** Phase 2: 4/25 roles hold budget authority, none Class A
- **Tested in:** Guide `G-A`, question 6 — IIC Convener / Coordinator / ARIIA-NIRF nodal officer

**Refuting observation.** The Convener states they cannot initiate or influence any purchase, and that the Registrar does not consult them on this at all.

**If refuted.** There is no internal champion. Go-to-market must start with the Registrar as the entry point instead.

---

### H-06

**Claim.** Convener and Incubation Manager are often the same person.

- **Graded:** HYPOTHESIS
- **Source:** Phase 2 structural observation
- **Tested in:** Guide `G-A`, question 6 — IIC Convener / Coordinator / ARIIA-NIRF nodal officer

**Refuting observation.** They are separate people with separate spreadsheets, neither of whom sees the other's list.

**If refuted.** The 'one integration point' framing is wrong and the integration cost of any product is much higher than assumed.

---

### H-07

**Claim.** Rs 1.2 lakh is inside a plausible institutional budget range.

- **Graded:** HYPOTHESIS
- **Source:** Phase 0 anchor: LabelNest verified Rs 75,000/yr
- **Tested in:** Guide `G-B`, question 3 — Registrar / Dean (ICSR) / Controller of Examinations-adjacent office

**Refuting observation.** The Registrar describes any purchase at this level as requiring a committee, a budget cycle wait, and a category that innovation software does not cleanly fit into.

**If refuted.** The price band is wrong and must move materially - probably down, and probably to a different billing period.

---

### H-08

**Claim.** Institutions manage cohorts, so cohort tracking is a real need.

- **Graded:** HYPOTHESIS
- **Source:** P-A03, graded HYPOTHESIS
- **Tested in:** Guide `G-A`, question 6 — IIC Convener / Coordinator / ARIIA-NIRF nodal officer

**Refuting observation.** There is no cohort; startups are handled individually as they arrive, and nobody has ever grouped them.

**If refuted.** Drop cohort management from the product entirely. Absence from reports was absence of concept, not absence of data.

---

### H-09

**Claim.** IPR tracking is a real institutional problem, not just a documentation gap.

- **Graded:** SINGLE-SOURCE
- **Source:** P-A07 DOCUMENTED CRITICAL - no institution has a patent deadline calendar
- **Tested in:** Guide `G-A`, question 5 — IIC Convener / Coordinator / ARIIA-NIRF nodal officer

**Refuting observation.** IPR is handled by an outside agency or a TTO, deadlines are tracked on individual researchers' own systems, and the institute itself never tracks a patent.

**If refuted.** The CRITICAL finding is a reporting artefact. The patent module loses its justification.

---

### H-10

**Claim.** On-premise / data-localisation is a near-universal procurement condition.

- **Graded:** HYPOTHESIS
- **Source:** Phase 0 inference from competitor filtering
- **Tested in:** Guide `G-C`, question 3 — Purchase Committee member / IT Administrator / Finance officer

**Refuting observation.** A cloud SaaS product with no on-premise option clears procurement without objection, because the data is non-sensitive.

**If refuted.** The deployment constraint is soft and the vendor list narrows sharply.

## Rule

A refutation is recorded as loudly as a confirmation. `Falsification_Matrix.md` is not a sales document — if four of these ten die, the roadmap changes and that is the point of running it.
