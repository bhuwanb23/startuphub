# Interview Protocol

Built 2026-10-19 by `build/build_phase3b.py`.

---

## Before you book

1. Read the guide for the role class you are interviewing.
2. Check the `hook` on each question — know what finding it tests, so you can follow up when the answer is thin.
3. Confirm the respondent is the person who **does the work**, not the person who signs it. A Convener who delegates the form to a junior gives you the wrong answer.
4. Have `Capture_Template.md` open. Have a copy of that institution's documented contradiction ready — you will be tempted to prime them with it, and you must not. Ask, then check.

---

## Timing — 30 to 40 minutes

| Minutes | Block |
|---:|---|
| 0–5 | Consent. Read `Consent_And_Ethics.md` verbatim. Do not paraphrase. |
| 5–20 | Core questions (bank priority PRIMARY for this role) |
| 20–30 | Secondary questions |
| 30–37 | **The artefact ask** — see below |
| 37–40 | Close: what did we get wrong? Anything you would add? |

### The artefact ask

The single highest-value request in the whole programme:

> *"Would you be willing to share a screenshot or a blank version of what you filled in last time? Blank out any names. I just want to see the shape of it — which columns you had open, where the data came from."*

This is how `P-A10` stops being a hypothesis. Phase 1 has only ever seen institutions' **exports**. The tool upstream of the export has never been observed by anyone on this project.

> **This is the one place a "would you" phrasing is correct.** It asks permission to receive a document — it is a consent request, not a validation question, and it asks nothing about the future. The rule bans hypothetical *purchase* intent; it does not ban asking for consent.

## Rules in the room

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Ask "walk me through the last time" | Ask "how do you usually" |
| Let the silence run after a question | Fill the silence yourself |
| Record non-answers as findings | Push past "I don't know" |
| Ask what actually happened | Ask what should happen |
| Take the contradiction seriously | Use it to catch them out |
| Ask for a number, then accept "roughly" | Insist on precision |

**On the documented contradiction.** We know, for example, that one institution reported 55 and 17 startups in the same PDF. We must **not** open with it. Ask the workflow questions cold. Only if the contradiction comes up unprompted do we probe it. If it never comes up, that is itself the finding — the institution does not know its own numbers.

## Immediately after — 10 minutes

1. Fill `Capture_Template.md` while it is fresh.
2. Tag every answer with a code from `Coding_Scheme.md`.
3. Mark any question where the respondent **could not answer**. Silence is data.
4. Note which hypotheses this interview confirms, refutes, or leaves open.
5. For paired institutions, write the divergence note before you forget it.

---

## What not to do

- **Do not demo the product.** No pilot, no screens, no pricing. We are measuring the current workflow, not selling.
- **Do not ask whether they would buy.** Ask what they last paid for and what head it came from.
- **Do not promise anything.** This is research.
- **Do not correct a wrong number** they give. Record it. A wrong number is the finding.
