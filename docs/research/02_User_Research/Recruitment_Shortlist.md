# Recruitment Shortlist

Built 2026-10-19 by `build/build_phase3b.py`. **6 institutions × 2 paired roles = 12 interviews.**

> ## The ranking rule is inverted on purpose
>
> A better interview target is one whose documentary record **contradicts itself**. A contradiction is a live question; a clean annual report is not. We are not chasing the most impressive institutions — we are chasing the ones where something visibly does not add up, because the officer who produced that can explain it, or cannot, and both are informative.

---

## Targets

### 1. NIT Calicut

- **Interview 1:** IIC Coordinator / Convener
- **Interview 2:** Registrar or Dean ICSR
- **Tests:** H-01, H-03, H-04, H-05

**Documented contradiction.** 55 vs 17 startups in one PDF; Rs 1,51,488 vs Rs 16,00,000 awareness spend (P-A08)

**Why this institution.** The single largest documented internal contradiction in the corpus. Both halves of that discrepancy were produced by the same office in the same document, so the Convener can explain it - or cannot, which is equally informative.

---

### 2. NIT Andhra Pradesh

- **Interview 1:** ARIIA/NIRF nodal officer
- **Interview 2:** Registrar
- **Tests:** H-02, H-01, H-03

**Documented contradiction.** 6 startups recognised, TRL evidence for only 2 - the worst join rate measured (67%)

**Why this institution.** The Phase 3A headline has a live example. Asking this officer directly where the other 4 DPIIT numbers went is the single highest-value question in the whole programme.

---

### 3. NIT Warangal

- **Interview 1:** ARIIA/NIRF nodal officer
- **Interview 2:** Registrar or IIC President
- **Tests:** H-02, H-05

**Documented contradiction.** TRL table carries a DPIIT number absent from the recognised table (reverse gap)

**Why this institution.** A contradiction in the opposite direction - the data exists but is filed under a key nobody recognised. Tests whether anyone reconciles the two tables (OQ-34).

---

### 4. Teresa's College

- **Interview 1:** IIC Coordinator
- **Interview 2:** Principal or Registrar
- **Tests:** H-04, H-03, H-01

**Documented contradiction.** A 13-item grant narrative inserted into a field meant for a number (P-A02)

**Why this institution.** The most vivid evidence of form abuse in the corpus, and the officer who typed it can describe the pressure. Best case for H-04 - is the deadline actually forcing bad data?

---

### 5. Dr. N.G.P. Arts and Science College

- **Interview 1:** IIC Coordinator
- **Interview 2:** Principal
- **Tests:** H-01, H-08, H-03

**Documented contradiction.** Grant figure reconciles to two unrelated DBT grants - aggregation presented as one number (P-A09)

**Why this institution.** Tests whether institutions can state what a rupee of government money actually funded. Directly probes the stock-vs-flow problem behind P-A06.

---

### 6. SSCET

- **Interview 1:** IIC Coordinator
- **Interview 2:** Principal or Dean
- **Tests:** H-08, H-03, H-06

**Documented contradiction.** 40 IIC members, 30 ambassadors, 2 mentors - ratio implies capacity, not pipeline

**Why this institution.** A high-volume institution with an implausibly thin mentoring base. Best case for testing H-08 (are cohorts real?) and H-03 (what is the 2-mentor process actually doing).

## Pairing checklist

Before an institution counts as done, both roles must have been interviewed. Compare on:

1. How many startups does the institute have? Do the two numbers match?
2. Who owns the startup data?
3. What was the last thing the institute paid for in this space?
4. Who could actually approve a purchase here?

**A divergence is the finding**, not a data-quality problem. It tells us whether the person with the pain can reach the person with the money.
