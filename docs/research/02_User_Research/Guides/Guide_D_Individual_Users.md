**Role class D · recruitment priority 4 · Phase 2 refs: D-01, D-02**

## Why this role, and why now

They are the source of the DPIIT number and the person who experiences the missing record, but they cannot buy anything. The buying bank is skipped to protect interview time.

## Questions

### 1. After your startup was recognised, did anyone at the institute ask you for anything - a form, a number, a report?

- **Bank:** Q1 — Current workflow
- **If the answer is thin, ask:** Did you get asked for your DPIIT number? Did you have to dig for it?
- **Tests:** `OQ-32`
- **This answer would show:** Tests the founder side of the join-key problem.

### 2. Is there any place you can see what stage your own startup is at, that the institute also sees?

- **Bank:** Q2 — Pain
- **If the answer is thin, ask:** Or is it only the institute that tracks it?
- **Tests:** `P-A06 / P-A01`
- **This answer would show:** P-A06 grades stock-not-movement DOCUMENTED.

### 3. When your institute filed a report that mentioned you, did it get anything wrong?

- **Bank:** Q2 — Pain
- **If the answer is thin, ask:** Did you correct it? Did anyone notice?
- **Tests:** `P-A02 / P-A06`
- **This answer would show:** Tests whether the ledger is wrong about real startups.

### 4. What do you personally use to keep track of your startup right now?

- **Bank:** Q3 — Existing tools
- **If the answer is thin, ask:** Notes app? WhatsApp? Google Sheets? Nothing?
- **Tests:** `Phase 0 free-incumbent assumption`
- **This answer would show:** Tests the 'free incumbent' claim from the user side.

## The artefact ask

> *"Would you be willing to share a screenshot or a blank version of what you filled in last time? Blank out any names. I just want to see the shape of it — which columns you had open, where the data came from."*

Optional, at the end. Declining is fine and is not a signal about the institution.

> *Note:* this asks permission to receive a document. It is a consent request, not a validation question — it hypothesises nothing about the future, which is why it is allowed to be phrased this way when the structured questions are not.
