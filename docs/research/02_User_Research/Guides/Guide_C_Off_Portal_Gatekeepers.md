## Why this role, and why now

They control the money and the security gate but do not do the work, so workflow questions are wasted slots. Phase 2 found the IT Administrator appears in ZERO procurement instruments by name.

## Questions

### 1. What falls below a committee, and what goes to it?

- **Bank:** Q4 — Buying
- **If the answer is thin, ask:** Is there a de-minimis threshold? Has it changed?
- **Tests:** `Phase 2 procurement ladders`
- **This answer would show:** Confirms or breaks the documented Rs 50K-Rs 10L spread.

### 2. If a vendor must store venture data on their own servers, who signs that off?

- **Bank:** Q4 — Buying
- **If the answer is thin, ask:** Has anyone ever refused a purchase on security grounds?
- **Tests:** `OQ-30`
- **This answer would show:** Resolves whether IT Admin has a real veto.

### 3. What security documents do you require from a software vendor?

- **Bank:** Q3 — Existing tools
- **If the answer is thin, ask:** SOC 2? ISO 27001? Data localisation? Government cloud only?
- **Tests:** `Phase 0 competitor disqualification (EcoSync)`
- **This answer would show:** Tests whether on-premise/data-localisation is universal.

## The artefact ask

> *"Would you be willing to share a screenshot or a blank version of what you filled in last time? Blank out any names. I just want to see the shape of it — which columns you had open, where the data came from."*

Optional, at the end. Declining is fine and is not a signal about the institution.

> *Note:* this asks permission to receive a document. It is a consent request, not a validation question — it hypothesises nothing about the future, which is why it is allowed to be phrased this way when the structured questions are not.
