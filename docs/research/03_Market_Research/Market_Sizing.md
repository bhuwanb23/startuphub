## Conflict C-05 — the same market sized 625× apart

| Research house | Size | Year | Forecast | CAGR |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 6Wresearch | US$ 499.0M | 2025 | US$ 718.0M (2032) | 6.5% |
| Grand View Research | US$ 0.8M | 2024 | US$ 3.4M (2030) | 27.7% |

**That is a 624× disagreement** between two research houses, on the same market, in the same currency.

> A 625x disagreement between two research houses on the SAME market in the SAME currency. At least one is measuring something else - possibly India-only vs India+APAC, or software-only vs total.

**Our choice.** Use NEITHER as a denominator. They are recorded to show that published market sizing in this category is unusable, which is itself a reason to build bottom-up from institution counts and budgets - which is what we do.

## Conflict C-04 — institution counts differ by frame, not by error

| Source | Colleges | Universities |
|---|---:|---:|
| AISHE 2021-22 | 45,473 | 1,168 |
| IBEF FY26 | 52,461 | 1,409 |
| NITI Feb 2025 | ~46,000 affiliates of 495 SPUs | 495 SPUs |

> Not a data error - three defensible frames. AISHE counts REGISTERED institutions; IBEF counts a later year on a different collection; NITI counts only affiliates of State Public Universities, which is a subset defined by funding route.

**Our choice.** Use AISHE for registered counts and NITI for the state-public system, always naming the frame. Never mix frames in one calculation.
