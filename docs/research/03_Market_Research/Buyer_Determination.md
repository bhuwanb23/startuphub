# Which institutions are actually potential buyers

**Phase 5 — built 2026-10-19.** The spec's instruction: *"Don't automatically add all of these together. You need to determine which ones are actually potential buyers."*

> Modelled as a **funnel**, because each stage strictly filters the one above it. Adding these numbers produces 1,19,477, which is nonsense — it counts the same institution four times.

---

| Stage | Population | Basis | A buyer population? |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1. Registered higher education institutions | 58,643 | AISHE 2021-22 | no |
| 2. Institutions in the state-public system | 46,000 | NITI Aayog Feb 2025 (affiliates of 495 SPUs) | no |
| 3. Institutions holding an IIC / AIC / EIC | 14,067–16,367 | Three official sources - see C-01 | no |
| 4. Institutions demonstrably filing innovation returns | 777 | NIRF India Rankings 2025, Innovation category | **YES — this one** |

**Stage 1 — Registered higher education institutions**  
Super-bound. Counts registration, not innovation function, not willingness.  
*Why it is not the answer:* 0 of these are addressable on this number alone.

**Stage 2 — Institutions in the state-public system**  
81% of all student enrolment sits here, funded by STATE governments.  
*Why it is not the answer:* Barely appears in the Union budget, so its financials are separately BOUNDED.

**Stage 3 — Institutions holding an IIC / AIC / EIC**  
Has a mandated innovation reporting function. Still registration, not behaviour.  
*Why it is not the answer:* An IIC registered but never submitting is a logo, not a buyer.

**Stage 4 — Institutions demonstrably filing innovation returns**  
OBSERVED BEHAVIOUR, not registration. A filing is something an institution actually did. This is the only stage we treat as a real buyer population.  
*Why it is not the answer:* Still not willingness to pay. This is a ceiling of who COULD buy, not who WILL.

---

## 777 is a ceiling of who *could* buy, not who *will*

Stage 4 is the only stage we treat as a real population, because a NIRF Innovation filing is **observed behaviour** — something an institution actually did, not something it registered for.

It is still not willingness to pay. The funnel gets us to *who is capable of being a buyer*. What they will pay remains `OQ-03`, unmeasured after five phases.

---

## Disqualifiers — who we should NOT sell to

A funnel that only describes who to sell to will eventually be used to sell to someone who cannot buy.

- IIC registered but no report ever submitted - F-SENTINEL-ZERO shows institutions record 0 for real activity
- No ARIIA/NIRF nodal officer named anywhere in any published material
- Files NIRF Overall but not Innovation - active in the ecosystem, absent from the ledger that matters
- 'No Data Found'-heavy forms - F-NODF-GRADIENT shows blankness tracks immaturity
- Conversion Manager delegates the form to a junior - we would be selling to the wrong person (Phase 4 G4)

## The designated primary segment — and its honest financial bound

Colleges are 65.3% private unaided and the primary target by user direction. **They have no published itemised finances anywhere.** Rather than guess:

> **Estimate (BOUNDED):** Revenue and discretionary spend per private unaided college: Rs 40 lakh - Rs 400 crore per year

**Method.** Three anchors, none of them a published college balance sheet. (1) University-level study of 8 state universities found fee collection exceeding 50% of total income at 3 of 8, ranging to 0.93% - so fee income dominates and varies 20x. (2) The only private university line in the entire DoHE budget is Rs 604 crore across 10 deemed universities = Rs 60 crore each. (3) 65.3% of colleges are private unaided with no public funding line at all. The band spans the observed fee-income variation and is deliberately wide.

**Caveat.** Our list price is 0.3% of the LOW end of this band and 0.00003% of the high end. Even in the worst case the product is affordable. This reinforces the finding: PROCESS, not price.

**What would narrow it:** One college's audited annual report, or a single Registrar naming a budget head (OQ-29).

- Union Budget 2025-26 - private deemed university allocation
- AISHE 2021-22 - 65.3% private unaided
