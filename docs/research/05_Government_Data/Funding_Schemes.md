# Funding Schemes and State Policies

**Phase 6. Built 2026-10-19.** This page corrects a Phase 5 conclusion.

---

## The correction

Phase 5 concluded:

> *"Only one of these points forward in time — AIM's grant-in-aid. ARIIA's expenditure split is retrospective. For anyone selling into this ecosystem, that is the difference between a budget you can plan around and one you can only read after the fact."*

**That was based on the funding pools Phase 5 had collected. It was incomplete.** There are four central incubator schemes, and at least three of them flow directly to degree institutions.

---

## Central incubator schemes

| Scheme | Ministry | Max grant | Duration | Reaches institutions |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| **TIDE 2.0** | MeitY | **Rs 27.2 cr** | 5 yr | **yes** |
| **AIC (Atal Incubation Centre)** | AIM / NITI Aayog | **Rs 10 cr** | 5 yr | **yes** |
| **BioNest** | DBT / BIRAC | not published | 3-5 yr | **yes** |
| **TBI (Technology Business Incubator)** | DST / NSTEDB | not published | 5 yr | **yes** |

**TIDE 2.0** — Academic/R&D institutions and incubators with industry linkages; 3 tiers G1C/G2C/G3C. Largest incubator grant found anywhere in this research.

**AIC (Atal Incubation Centre)** — Public-funded, private or PPP institutions; 10,000 sq ft built-up space minimum. Already mined in Phase 5.

**BioNest** — Academic institutes, research hospitals, existing incubators; private partners contribute 20% capex. Grant amount is project-dependent; not published as a ceiling.

**TBI (Technology Business Incubator)** — Not-for-profit entity; host institution must exist 3+ years. Grant amount not stated in the public guidelines.

> **TIDE 2.0's Rs 27.2 crore ceiling is the largest incubator grant found in this research** — more than double AIM's Rs 10 crore, and it is not in any Phase 5 document.

## State policy examples

| State | Provision | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Assam** | 75% grant, capped at Rs 5 crore, for incubation centres | IITs, engineering colleges, universities, biotechnology parks, IT parks, educational institutions |
| **Bihar** | Incubators receive 3% of incubatees' SEBI-registered AIF investment; government matches 1:1 | State-supported incubators |
| **Tamil Nadu** | Land at nominal lease for 99 years for Startup Parks | Entrepreneurs including NRIs |
| **Puducherry** | Land or built-up space at preferential rates for startup cells | Startup cells in industrial estates and IT parks |

> **Assam's is the sharpest:** a 75% grant capped at Rs 5 crore, explicitly for incubation centres **at IITs, engineering colleges and universities**. That is forward-looking capital aimed squarely at the Phase 4 G2 segment.

---

## What this changes

1. **The 'no forward-looking budget signal' finding was wrong.** Institutions and incubators at them are grant recipients under at least three central schemes.
2. **The grant is tied to infrastructure and incubation, not software.** A Rs 5 crore incubator grant is not a Rs 1.2 lakh software budget. The software has to attach to a grant-funded programme, which is a different sale.
3. **`OQ-03` is still open.** Grant availability says an institution *could* spend. It says nothing about what it would spend on innovation software. Nothing here changes the price-band decision.
