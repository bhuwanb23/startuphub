## Why tier-spread, and not the winners

Phase 1 learned what happens when a corpus is made of institutions that volunteer their own success: the winners are visible and the failures are not. That is recorded as OQ-21.

Mining only Gujarat and Tamil Nadu would repeat the same error at state level. So the target list was deliberately spread across all five SRF tiers.

> ### ZERO Emerging-tier state reports could be obtained. The tier that needs this product most is the least documented in public data.

Six states were attempted across the missing tiers (Odisha, Meghalaya, Puducherry, Goa, Chhattisgarh, NCT of Delhi). Filename variants were tried for each; every one returned an HTML error page rather than a PDF.

**We are not filling that gap with an assumption.** It stays recorded as zero until someone re-verifies it. The build guard fails if this figure is changed without re-checking.

## What the mined reports show

**Gujarat** (Best Performer, A1) — One of the first movers in setting up a startup portal; average grievance resolution seven days (per SRF 2022 summary).

**Tamil Nadu** (Top Performer, A1) — Bilingual portal (English + Tamil). Query resolution reported as ~15 days in the 2022 summary.

**Himachal Pradesh** (Top Performer, B) — Category B but runs a Rs 10 crore seed fund - a forward-looking budget signal, unusual for a sub-1-crore-population state.

**Telangana** (Leader, A2) — Portal name not stated in the report text we extracted.

**Kerala** (Leader, A2) — Fell from Best Performer (2022) to Leader (5.0). No probe hit in our extraction.

**Bihar** (Aspiring Leader, A1) — Largest Category A state by population, an Aspiring Leader. Policy offers incubators 3% of incubatee SEBI AIF investment, matched 1:1 by government.

Two figures stand out:

- **Gujarat lists 250 incubators.** A state maintaining an incubator directory of that size has an information-management problem that is not a spreadsheet problem.
- **Himachal Pradesh runs a Rs 10 crore seed fund** despite being a Category B state (population under 1 crore). Category is a poor proxy for budget.
