| Edition | Year | Reform areas | Action points | Marks | Participating | Complete |

**38 → 25 → 19 action points, and marks cut from 100 to 75**, while participation rose from ~30 to 34 States and UTs. The framework got narrower as it got broader.

| **Rajasthan** | Top Performer | Leader | 1 |
| **Telangana** | Top Performer | Leader | 1 |
| **Assam** | Leader | Aspiring Leader | 1 |

> **Kerala and Odisha each fell two tiers; six more fell one.** Gujarat has been a Best Performer continuously since 2018 — the only state with that record in the published results.

**What this tells StartupHub:** state ecosystem quality is not a fixed ranking. A state at the top in 2022 can be a Leader in 2026, which means the need is not static and a one-off state sale is not a durable position. It also means **emerging states are where relative improvement is easiest** — and they are the ones with no published evidence at all.

## The scoring rubric we can borrow

SRF scores states on mentorship with explicit bands. We can score our own 36-document corpus on the same scale:

| Band | Category A (>1 crore pop) | Category B (<1 crore pop) |
|---|---|---|
| Score 3 | >80 mentors | >20 mentors |
| Score 2 | 41-80 mentors | 10-20 mentors |
| Score 1 | 1-40 mentors | 1-9 mentors |

**2 of 36 institutions (5.6%) can be scored on this rubric.** Where we can:

| Institution | Mentors | Category A | Category B |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| National Institute of Technology Calicut | 29 | 1/3 | 3/3 |
| Vijaya Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences for Women | 5 | 1/3 | 1/3 |

> **The limit is the data, not the rubric.** Phase 1 populates `faculty_mentors` in only 2 of 36 institutions. The government's own metric is the right one and we cannot apply it — which is itself the strongest argument for the product.
