## 5. The price band StartupHub operates in

```
₹0        ₹54k        ₹75k        ₹1.2L      ₹3.95L     ₹5.4L        ₹44.6L
│          │           │           │          │          │            │
│          │           │           │          │          │            └─ Elsevier Pure (small Indian university)
│          │           │           │          │          └─ AcceleratorApp core (~$499/mo)
│          │           │           │          └─ Incubatee year one
│          │           │           └─ todo.md's unvalidated ₹1.2L assumption
│          │           └─ 🔴 Orbit Launch — the competitive anchor
│          └─ Trello at 10 users — the free-tier floor
└─ Excel, Sheets, WhatsApp, campus ERP's marginal cost = ₹0
```

**Above ₹5.4L/yr** we are competing with Elsevier Pure and enterprise innovation suites on their turf.
**Below ₹54k/yr** we cannot survive against Trello's free tier.

**The defensible middle is ₹75k–₹5L**, and within it the only axis that matters is *the only thing that produces a compliant submission* — not price, not feature count.

## 6. What drives price across this market — four patterns

| Pattern | Who does it | Implication for StartupHub |
|---|---|---|
| **Per-user / per-seat** | AcceleratorApp (+$49/Funding seat), Incubatee (₹850/admin/mo), Trello, Asana, Monday, ClickUp | Penalises exactly the population an incubator has most of — founders, students, mentors. |
| **Per-team / flat** | Orbit (per applications + seats + data rooms), RiserNest Model A, LoftOS, EcoSync | Predictable for the institution; the admin is one person. |
| **No per-user at all** | **VentureLink** ("No per-user fees. No hidden feature gating") | The structural counter-position. Fewest vendors offer it. |
| **Volume-gated** | VentureLink add-ons, incuTrack modules, RiserNest teams | Price rises with success — which institutions distrust. |

> **Insight from the Indian ERP context:** an institution's instinct is to distrust any vendor whose price scales with its success, because the per-student ERP model trains them to see it that way. **A flat institutional price is genuinely differentiated** — but it must be *defensible*, not just cheap.

## 7. Gaps in pricing research

1. **No Indian institution has been asked.** Zero willingness-to-pay data. → `OQ-03`
2. **EcoSync's actual prices** — page is client-side rendered, requires account. Cannot confirm the claim that "the entry price is real and published."
3. **Gohorto's actual prices** — JS-only pricing page; GetApp's $399 is third-party and unconfirmed.
4. **AcceleratorApp core** — sales-gated; only the Funding module is published.
5. **Procurement overhead is unmodelled.** GeM listing fees, security-questionnaire labour, and committee time are real costs to the buyer and are not in any published price. → `OQ-13`
6. **No pilot pricing.** Nobody publishes what a 4–8 week pilot costs, which is exactly the Phase 20 validation offer StartupHub needs.
7. **All G2/Capterra-derived prices are search-index excerpts** — both sites 403 automated fetch. Re-verify before customer-facing use.
