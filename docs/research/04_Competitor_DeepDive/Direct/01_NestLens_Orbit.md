## What it does NOT do

- ❌ **No IPR or patent tracking.** Nothing on patents, trademarks, copyrights, invention disclosures, IP ownership, or technology transfer.
- ❌ **No TRL modelling.** Tracks "sector, stage, revenue, headcount" — not readiness levels. YUKTI-NIR explicitly grades by TRL bands.
- ❌ **No office space / lab / facility booking.** It is cohort-and-money, not real estate.
- ❌ **No IIC / YUKTI-NIR / ARIIA submission flow.** No mention of MoE's Innovation Cell, YUKTI-NIR, IIC registration or ARIIA ranking data.
- ❌ **No on-premise / data-residency option** stated.
- ❌ No educational curriculum/LMS of its own.

### 🔴 Strategic read

Orbit is the vendor that has **already done most of what `todo.md` Phase 0 proposed as V1** — incubation management, mentor tracking, programmes, milestones, performance, reports. At ₹75,000/yr it is priced *below* the ₹1.2L assumption in `todo.md`.

**This is the single most important competitive fact in the project.** It means:
- "we'll build an incubation platform" is **not** a viable beachhead on its own
- The wedge must be somewhere Orbit structurally cannot go — which by elimination is **IPR, TRL, and the mandated-portal export** (see `Positioning_Map` Gaps 1–3)
- `Decision_Log` `D-006`: ₹75,000/yr is the price StartupHub will be compared against
