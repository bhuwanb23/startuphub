## The services-business model

SCIP also runs **back-office outsourcing**:
> "We Establish Best-in-class Incubation Processes for Clients... **Don't outsource deal selection and incubatee investment decisions.**"

Plus a bundled tool suite that removes SaaS sprawl:
- **SCIPpoint** — scheduling
- **HiLegally** — contract drafting/negotiation
- **FoldersFiles** — file sharing
- **SCIPsign** — e-sign
- **SCIPnews** — news parsing
- **iCount** — private communities (forthcoming)

Security claims: ISO 27001, SSP, 2FA. Languages: English, Hindi. Android mobile supported.

**Founder credibility:** co-founder Manoj was COO of Dubai SME and created Dubai's government incubator plus its seed-investing programme (2005–2013). Genuine domain depth.

## 🔴 Strategic read

Incubatee proves two things simultaneously:

1. **An India-first vendor can charge ₹3 lakh and still be undercut 4×.** The price ceiling is not the ceiling — it is one vendor's ceiling.
2. **The services layer is a real business model.** *"Platform + back-office management"* is a pricing and delivery architecture StartupHub has not considered. It also solves the migration problem (`Positioning_Map` Gap 10) by doing the work rather than selling a tool.

The "don't outsource deal selection and incubatee investment decisions" line is a deliberate scope boundary — it shows the vendor understands exactly which decisions an incubator will never delegate. Any StartupHub services offering should respect the same boundary.
