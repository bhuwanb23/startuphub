## 🔴 Strategic read

Accubate is the **credibility benchmark** and simultaneously the **channel risk**:

- It proves a **non-technical Indian buyer will sign a contract** for incubation management software — worth more than any feature comparison
- Its strongest differentiators (white-label, multi-site) are exactly what StartupHub would need to match to sell to large multi-campus institutions
- **Its weakness is documentation and on-site clarity.** The only verifiable complaint in the entire India cohort is *"No User manual!!"* — which suggests a real, cheap, winnable differentiation: **onboarding quality, in-product guidance, and migration support** (`Positioning_Map` Gap 10)
- **The conference-management DNA is visible.** Its strongest customer is Hero MotoCorp's *Government Administration* function, not its incubation arm. That is a warning that the product may be bought for events, not for venture management — and that positioning language should not drift toward "events"
