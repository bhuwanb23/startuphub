# EcoSync — Dossier

**The most operationally localised competitor in India — and the most procurement-vulnerable.**
Vendor: **Opernova Technologies LLP**, India (city not disclosed) · `COMP-003`

## Positioning (quoted)

> "EcoSync is incubation management software for incubators, accelerators and technology business incubators. One white-labelled portal for applications, weighted panel evaluation, office space, facilities, funding and reporting — with an audit trail on every decision."

Two surfaces: **ecosync.co** (incubator product) and **ecosync.network** (public founder/mentor/investor network).
⚠️ Tracxn lists a separate unrelated "Ecosync" at ecosync.in — a pollution-control company. **Do not conflate.**

## Pricing 🔴 NOT PUBLIC — requires sign-in

Their pricing page says prices are *"read from the live plan catalogue when the page loads, so it cannot drift from what you are actually charged"* — but the table is client-side rendered and returned no figures. Their own FAQ admits it:

> "Why are the higher tiers not listed here? ... a full price table published here would be wrong for most of the people reading it. The entry price is real and published. The tier that fits you is a short conversation."

**Price drivers (stated):** team accounts, startups held, document storage, active modules. Monthly, yearly or weekly billing. Pilot runs with defined start/end dates, converting to paid.
**Free tier:** free for individuals (founders, mentors, investors, students) — profile, posts, communities, messaging, events, applications, mentor booking, job applications. No card required.

🔴 **Procurement problem in India:** institutions need a budget quote for approval. A "short conversation" is a real friction point against a purchase committee.

## India-specific depth — the deepest of any competitor found

> "EcoSync defaults to INR. Platform fees, GST and TDS are calculated per transaction rather than reconciled afterwards. Payouts reach a bank account or a UPI ID. Scheduling assumes Asia/Kolkata."

Plus **Razorpay Route** payouts, and a funding model of **source → programme allocation → tranche disbursed to startup**, with **over-allocation blocked at entry**.

Also: five office-space pricing tiers with deposits, recurring rent and Razorpay payouts; day-of-week time-slot facility booking with approval workflow; **five mentor payment models** including subsidised and retainer.

## 🔴 The most candid vendor statement in the entire research

> "**We do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification**, and we would rather tell you that here than have you find out in a security questionnaire. **We do not publish an uptime SLA.** We are a small team in India building this for incubators in India, and **our first incubator is still our reference customer rather than our hundredth.**"

**This is a strategic gift.** EcoSync is arguably the *best-placed* competitor operationally — and it has pre-emptively answered "no" to the two questions a university IT or procurement committee asks first.

## EcoSync's AI counter-position — worth stealing

Even with no AI product, EcoSync articulates the problem better than anyone with AI:

> "Panel evaluation is where spreadsheets do the most damage, because the damage is invisible. **Two evaluators scoring out of ten with no shared rubric produce numbers that look comparable and are not.**"

That is a precise description of the failure mode an institution cannot currently detect. It is also a **defensible AI thesis**: for Indian institutions, AI that cannot produce a citation trail is *worse than no AI*, because it destroys the audit defensibility that panel evaluation exists to provide. → `Positioning_Map` Gap 8
