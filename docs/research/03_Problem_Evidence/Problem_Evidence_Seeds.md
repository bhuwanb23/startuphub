## The 12 problem areas

### 1. Startup tracking · 🟡 INFERRED

**Evidence.** The IIC portal has **no venture-entity module whatsoever** — its 17 menus contain no CRM, pipeline or portfolio object (`GOV-018`). NIRF asks for startups as *rows* — name, DPIIT number, funding amounts, year — **per discipline, per year** (`GOV-028`). YUKTI-NIR holds a *snapshot* in three buckets (`GOV-023`).

> **Inference:** the only place a venture exists as a trackable entity over time is the institution's own private records — which by Phase 0 evidence is Excel or WhatsApp (`COMP-024`, `COMP-026`).

**Still needs validation.** How many institutions actually maintain a venture register today, in what format, updated how often, by whom.

---

### 2. Innovation activity tracking · 🟢 DOCUMENTARY

**Evidence — this is the most heavily evidenced problem area in the entire project.**
- **≥16 activities/year** minimum: 12 IIC Calendar (3/quarter) + 4 Celebration (1/quarter), **plus all MIC-driven activities** (`GOV-020`)
- Each activity requires: **four social media links** (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn), participant counts split three ways (student/faculty/external), **expenditure in rupees**, overview, objective, benefits, remark, **video URL, two photographs, and a document upload** (`GOV-021`)
- Levels 1–4 by contact hours determine scoring weight
- *"Conducting the event alone will not secure the stars / score. The activity you have conducted should be approved by the IIC."*

**And the hard gate.** Council meeting minutes must be uploaded for the quarter, minimum **two council meetings per academic year**, each with a signed Resolution in a prescribed format listing every member and their responsibilities, a quarterly plan, minutes with President and Convenor present, and a photograph. Verbatim from the portal manual:

> *"If it is in red cross then you cannot add any activity under 'Manage Activity' section."*
> *"If 'Add to My Calendar' button is not available under some events, that will be because… You would have not completed / uploaded 'Council Meeting' for that particular quarter."* (`GOV-019`)

> 🔴 **A governance artefact gates the reporting form.** This is the single hardest workflow constraint found anywhere in this research, and it is undocumented in any secondary source.

### 3. Student / faculty innovation management · 🟡 INFERRED

**Evidence.** MIC trained **60,000+ faculty** as Innovation Ambassadors (`GOV-007`); the IIC dashboard separately counts student council members, student idea submissions, and faculty Innovation Ambassadors. AY 2025-26 annual reports are self-labelled *"IIC 8.0"* — indicating the programme has versioned at least eight times, each with new tracking expectations.

**Gap.** No commercial platform has a first-class faculty or innovator entity — all eleven build from "startup application" (`Positioning_Map` Gap 6). Faculty are the **source** of everything downstream: inventions → disclosures → patents → licences → startups.

**Still needs validation.** Whether faculty innovation management is a felt pain or a compliance checkbox.

---

### 4. Incubation management · 🟢 DOCUMENTARY

**Evidence.** The IIC portal has **"Manage Pre-Incubation / Incubation Details"** — and it registers **the facility, not the incubatees**: floor-area and existence metadata for pre-incubation centres, incubation centres and IPR/TTO Centres (`GOV-022`, `GOV-018`). YUKTI-NIR grades innovations into TRL bands but holds no cohort.

Scale of the underlying estate: **3,249 IPR/TTO offices · 5,157 pre-incubation + 3,083 incubation facilities** (`GOV-007`); **103 AIC+ACIC centres incubating 6,782 startups** (`GOV-008`); **517+ MeitY Startup Hub incubators** (`GOV-012`).

> **Inference:** a national estate of thousands of registered facilities, none of which has a mandated system recording who is in them.

---

### 5. Mentorship coordination · 🟢 DOCUMENTARY

**Evidence.** The IIC portal's *"Manage Mentor-Mentee Program"* is a **scheme participation form** — no session calendar, no matching, no outcome capture, no goal tracking (`GOV-022`).

**By contrast, the most operationally capable system in the entire mandated stack is MAARG** — the national mentorship platform with AI matchmaking, session scheduling, cohort programmes and dashboards. But MAARG is **scoped to DPIIT-recognised startups, not institutional portfolios** (`GOV-041`).

**The compliance dimension nobody has.** In a university context mentors are:
- Often external → subject to conflict-of-interest declaration, NDA and IP non-disclosure **before** they see any pre-incubation idea
- Required to log attendance and hours **against scheme utilisation rules** (NIDHI/BIRAC/CSR fund mentors' time)
- Required to **sign a confidentiality agreement before seeing innovations** — exactly what AICTE's own YUKTI-NIR NDA templates address (*"PATENT / INVENTION NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT"*)
- Subject to **conflict checks** against their own IP and startup holdings

> **A single NDA leakage could void an IPR claim.** No platform separates "public mentor" from "confidential expert under NDA" visibility tiers, or logs mentorship as a fundable scheme cost.

### 6. IPR tracking · 🔴 **THE STRONGEST EVIDENCE IN THE PROJECT**

**This is the best-evidenced problem area we found, and it comes entirely from primary government sources.**

| Evidence | Source |
|---|---|
| The IIC portal registers an **"IPR/TTO Centre"** by existence and floor area — and has **no field to record a single patent** | `GOV-022` |
| NIRF Innovation requires **"Patents Commercialized & Technology Transferred"** and **"Income from Technology Transferred (in Rupees)"** by calendar year | `GOV-028` |
| ARIIA requires per innovation: **"Patent Application No"**, **"Year of Patent Application"**, **"Current Status"**, **"IP / Patented Technology"** — verified in a real institutional submission | `GOV-032` |
| KAPILA: **2,746 institutes** registered; **11,768 patents reimbursed** with ₹380 lakh funding; reimbursement raised to **100% (₹5,600)** for up to 100 patents/institution from FY 2024-25 | `GOV-007` |
| **National HEI patent filings rose 119% in two years** — 15,383 (FY23) → 33,675 (FY25) | `GOV-007` |
| MeitY's official playbook mandates IP due diligence: ownership clarity, **freedom to operate**, defensibility, and distinguishing ownership from a **limited-term (2-year) university tech-transfer licence** | `GOV-038` |
| IIT Madras runs an **IP Management Cell** covering *"early-stage disclosures and prior-art evaluation to drafting, prosecution and long-term portfolio management"*, filing **431 patents in one year** (352 international, 79 Indian), plus a TTO | `COMP-027` |
| NIDHI-PRAYAS requires an institutional **No Objection Certificate** confirming *"any intellectual property generated will vest entirely with the innovator or startup"* | `GOV-037` |
| **Zero of eleven direct competitors have any IPR module** | `Positioning_Map` Gap 1 |

> **The chain is complete and citable:** institutions are mandated to report patent-level data to three different bodies, filing patent applications at a 119% two-year growth rate, with a dedicated NOC requirement for IP vesting — and **no mandated system anywhere holds a patent record**. Institutions maintain a private register and re-type it by hand every year.
>
> **Note the one counter-signal.** In NIRF 2025, *"Patent Commercialization & Technology Transfer (10)" was **removed** from Innovation Achievements and replaced by *"Alumni Founders of Forbes/Fortune 500 (20)."* So the *scoring* incentive for patents fell even as filing volume rose. Worth understanding before betting everything on IPR.

### 7. Resource management · 🟡 INFERRED

**Evidence.** **3,249 IPR/TTO offices · 5,157 pre-incubation + 3,083 incubation facilities** registered with IIC — floor area only (`GOV-007`). ISERD/Atal labs: **10,000+ ATLs across 733 districts** (`GOV-009`); MeitY Startup Hub supports **329+ labs** (`GOV-012`).

**Competition.** This is the **one area with strong incumbents** — incuTrack does room inventory, key control, work orders, visitor/lobby management, lease and insurance tracking (`COMP-010`); EcoSync does five office-space pricing tiers with deposits, recurring rent, Razorpay payouts, and day-of-week slot booking with approval (`COMP-003`).

> **Strategic note:** this is where we should *not* compete (`Positioning_Map` §"where not to compete"). Resource management is also the **most likely source of a free/defensive feature** rather than a paid one.

### 8. Programme management · 🟢 DOCUMENTARY

**Evidence.** Gohorto's own framing captures the buyer pain precisely:
> *"Universities don't just run isolated programs; they manage a complex ecosystem that includes: → **Incubator → Accelerator → Hackathon → Entrepreneurship courses → Grant programs → Industry partnerships.** Each of these areas has different teams, budgets, and timelines."*

**Institutional scale.** Kerala operates **375+ IEDCs** across colleges and universities with 27 R&D institutions and 63 incubators (`GOV-042`) — a single state running hundreds of sub-institution programmes. IIC requires **16+ activities/year each with a full evidence submission** (`GOV-020`, `GOV-021`).

### 9. Startup performance monitoring · 🔴 ASSUMED

**Evidence — and it is a devastating negative.** The IIC dashboard's "Overall Impact" is defined as **total students and faculty who benefited from activities** — *event attendance headcount*, not venture outcomes. There is **no jobs, revenue, survival or graduation metric anywhere** in the mandated stack (`GOV-018`).

> **An institution cannot answer "how many of our incubatees are still alive?" from any government system.** This is the strongest possible statement of the gap — and simultaneously, this is the problem area with the **least primary evidence from us**. Nobody has yet been asked how they currently monitor performance. Phase 3 priority.

### 10. Reporting · 🟢 DOCUMENTARY

**This is where we have the most evidence, and it is documentary-grade.**

| Obligation | Scale |
|---|---|
| IIC council meetings | Min 2/yr, with signed resolutions, quarterly plans, minutes, photographs — **and they gate everything else** |
| IIC activities | **≥16/yr**, each a full evidence submission |
| YUKTI-NIR | Every innovation/startup, TRL-banded, institute-verified, **flows into ARIIA** |
| NIRF Innovation | **~11 screens per discipline**; per startup: name, DPIIT no, amount, funder, year, achievement level, investment type |
| ARIIA | **100+ indicators across 5 categories**, incl. patent-level fields |
| NIDHI / DST / BIRAC | Grant utilisation, audited certificates, action plans, **PFMS/CGA registration**, unspent-balance refund |
| AISHE | Binary NIRF participation status — plus the institution is the **registry** (AISHE code) everything else keys off |

**And no integration exists.** The only de-duplication is ARIIA's one-directional fetch from IIC/NISP/YUKTI — *"has reduced data submission efforts at the institute level by checking redundancy"* (`GOV-031`) — which does **not** cover NIRF-I and shows **no evidence of auto-population** in submitted NIRF forms.

🔴 **No public API, developer documentation or bulk export endpoint exists for IIC, YUKTI, NIRF, AISHE or MAARG** (`GOV-043`). All are form-based web apps with per-activity manual uploads.

**Corroborating signals of institutional friction:**
- AISHE self-declaration: *"the data is self-reported by the HEIs… the quality of data is generally taken care of by the respective HEIs"* (`GOV-033`)
- **77.4%** standalone-institution response — the weakest category — while government still claims ">90% overall" (`GOV-034`)
- AISHE 2024-25 had to be **reopened for data error rectification until 24 Dec 2025**; 2025-26 *and* 2026-27 registration were open simultaneously (`COMP-029`)
- India has **69,233 separate compliances and 6,618 independent filings** under ~1,500 Acts (`COMP-028`)

### 11. Ecosystem collaboration · 🟡 INFERRED

**Evidence.** Credential fragmentation is severe and documented:
- A single venture can hold **CIN → DIPP → KSUM UID → IIC ID → AISHE code → NIRF institute_id → ARIIA ID → SAKALA**. **No canonical identifier spans these.** (`GOV-025`, `GOV-042`)
- *"IIC login credentials and the YUKTI login credentials are different."*
- State portals are independent silos with their own registration, ID, login and form taxonomy, and **none exposes an API a system of record could write to.**
- 31 of 36 states have a startup policy, 27 developed after Startup India (`GOV-015`) — so 27+ separate policy frameworks the institution must track

**Data quality is already imperfect before any join.** Factly's government-derived dataset has **204 `institute_id` values against 202 `institute_name` values** — a 0.98% dirty-record rate (`GOV-030`).

## Phase 3 interview priorities, derived from this evidence

Ranked by how much the answer would change the thesis:

1. **Performance monitoring** (`#9`) — the weakest evidence and the strongest structural claim. Ask: *"How do you currently know how many of our startups are still active?"*
2. **IPR workflow** (`#6`) — best-documented problem, but we have never watched one being done. Ask: *"Walk me through the last patent you filed. Who did what, in which order, over how long?"*
3. **Reporting time** (`#10`) — ask the value framing: *"If this problem disappeared tomorrow, what would that save you?"* Not *"would you pay ₹1.2 lakh?"*
4. **The IIC gate** (`#2`) — how much does the council-meeting dependency actually cost in practice, and is it resented or accepted?
5. **Existing tooling** — *"What do you currently pay for? What is free? What do you use Excel for? What do you use WhatsApp for?"*
6. **Buying** — *"Who approves new software? Who controls the budget? How long does procurement take?"* → `OQ-13`
7. **Migration** — *"Can we get your data out of your current files?"* The single biggest sale blocker (`Positioning_Map` Gap 10)
8. **Language** — *"Are your council meetings and mentor sessions in Malayalam/Tamil/Marathi?"* Tests `OQ-16` and whether Orbit's Sarvam transcription is a real wedge or a nice-to-have
